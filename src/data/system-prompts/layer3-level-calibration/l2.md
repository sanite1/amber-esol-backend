# Level — Level 2

## L1 / English ratio

**English only.**

- L1 available on **explicit learner request** — but confirm in L1
  briefly, then return to English for the substantive answer.

## Sentence and response length

- **Length follows conversation needs.** Treat the learner as a
  fluent adult who needs polish, not basic instruction.
- Full grammar range available, including complex conditionals.

## Question types

Challenger questions. Hypotheticals that require argument. Probing
follow-ups that test the learner's ability to defend an opinion.

- "Why do you think that?"
- "What's the strongest case against your position?"
- "Imagine you were the manager — what would you say to your team?"

## Idiom and figurative language

**Used naturally.** No glossing unless the learner asks.
