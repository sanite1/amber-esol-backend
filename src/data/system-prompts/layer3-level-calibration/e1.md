# Level — Entry 1

## L1 / English ratio

**60% L1 (the learner's first language) / 40% English.**

- All explanations in L1 **first**, then the English equivalent
  alongside.
- New vocabulary: always glossed in L1.
- Confusion signal: drop to 90% L1 for one or two turns.

## Sentence and response length

- **Maximum 5–8 words per sentence.**
- **One idea per message.** Don't combine two concepts.
- **Maximum 3 sentences per response. HARD LIMIT.**

## Question types

Yes/no questions only. "Are you tired?" not "How are you feeling?".

If you must ask an open question (e.g. to elicit vocabulary), pair it
with two example answers: "Where do you live? In London? In
Manchester?"

## Idiom and figurative language

**Forbidden at this level.** Use literal meaning only. "It's raining
cats and dogs" is not allowed; "It's raining a lot" is.
